Free chest X-ray pattern-recognition trainer for medical students, residents, and radiographers.

About

ThoraSwipe is a swipe-based educational tool designed to build rapid CXR interpretation skills through spaced repetition and immediate feedback.

Cases are curated from open-access published literature. All images are sourced from Wikimedia Commons under open licences (CC0, CC BY, and CC BY-SA).
