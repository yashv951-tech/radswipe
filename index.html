About

ThoraSwipe is a swipe-based educational tool designed to build rapid CXR interpretation skills through spaced repetition and immediate feedback.

Cases are curated from open-access published literature. All images are sourced from Wikimedia Commons under open licences (CC0, CC BY, and CC BY-SA).

Privacy Policy

ThoraSwipe does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

  • No account or login is required.
  • No cookies or tracking technologies are used.
  • Your session progress — case position, score, streak, and difficulty filter — is saved to your browser's localStorage so you can close the app and resume where you left off. This data never leaves your device.
  • Saved progress is automatically cleared when you complete a round or tap Restart. You can also clear it at any time via your browser's site data settings.
  • Anonymous page-view analytics are collected via Vercel Web Analytics — cookieless, no personal data, no fingerprinting. See Vercel's Analytics Privacy Policy.

External network requests are made to: Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org) for radiograph images; Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com) for typefaces; and Vercel Analytics (vitals.vercel-insights.com) for anonymised page-view data. Each service is subject to its own privacy policy.
